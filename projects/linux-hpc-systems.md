# Linux & HPC Systems Engineering

## Overview

This case study summarizes hands-on work with Linux-based research infrastructure and multi-node compute environments.

## Environment

- Multi-node HPC cluster
- Rocky Linux and Ubuntu systems
- SSH-based administration
- Slurm workload-management concepts and operations
- Research workloads and cluster services

## Responsibilities

### Linux administration
- Managed Linux hosts used for research and compute workloads.
- Worked with users, groups, permissions, services, packages, logs, and remote administration.
- Used command-line tools to diagnose system, process, disk, memory, and networking issues.
- Performed node-readiness checks and service validation.

### HPC operations
- Worked with multi-node compute infrastructure and Slurm-based scheduling environments.
- Inspected controller and worker/compute readiness.
- Investigated workload and service issues across nodes.
- Supported reproducible research workloads and infrastructure experiments.

### Networking and troubleshooting
- Diagnosed host connectivity, DNS, routing, ports, services, and cluster communication.
- Used Linux logs and service state to isolate failures.
- Followed structured troubleshooting from infrastructure layer through application layer.

## Tools & Technologies

`Linux` `Rocky Linux` `Ubuntu` `Bash` `SSH` `systemd` `Slurm` `Networking` `Git`

## What this demonstrates

This work demonstrates practical capability in Linux administration, HPC environments, infrastructure troubleshooting, and supporting research systems where reliability and reproducibility matter.
